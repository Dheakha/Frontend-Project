The Beginning of Tamil Music

Tamil music has a rich and beautiful history that has been carried forward through generations.

From traditional instruments and classical melodies to folk songs and ancient rhythms, music has always been an important part of Tamil culture.

These timeless traditions became the foundation for the many forms of Tamil music we enjoy today.
