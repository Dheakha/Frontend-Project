ABOUT ECHOES

Celebrating Tamil Music

Echoes of Tamil is a journey through the sounds, stories and traditions of Tamil music.

Our Purpose

Echoes of Tamil brings together different generations and styles of Tamil music in one place. From classical traditions to modern independent sounds, every melody has a story to tell.

Our Vision

Our goal is to celebrate the beauty of Tamil music and introduce its rich musical heritage to a new generation of listeners.

Our Journey

Music connects people across time and generations. Echoes of Tamil celebrates those connections through artists, albums, stories and unforgettable sounds.
